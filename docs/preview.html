MagicBand Reader — Soldering Wiring Map

ESP32-S3 N16R8 · RC522 · MAX98357A · NeoPixels · volume pot · program button
Before soldering: disconnect both USB cables and check every wire end-to-end with continuity mode. Your notes use blue for both RC522 MISO and RC522 RST, so label those two blue wires before attaching them.

Soldering checklist

  1. Build the common ground connection first.
  2. Connect the RC522 to 3.3V only, never the 5V rail.
  3. Connect MAX98357A VIN and NeoPixel red to the external 5V rail.
  4. Add the 10µF and 0.1µF capacitors across amp VIN/GND.
  5. Connect signal wires only after power and ground pass a continuity check.
  6. Check for shorts between 5V, 3.3V, and GND before plugging in USB.
  7. Power up without the speaker first; then attach the speaker after the board boots normally.

Two details worth pausing on

  • Verify Your handwritten list says RC522 RST is blue, while MISO is also blue. Do not choose by color alone.
  • Confirmed RC522 IRQ stays disconnected, but RST is connected to GPIO 4 because the current software uses a hardware reset line.
  • Confirmed The two MAX98357A supply bypass capacitors are 10µF + 0.1µF in parallel between 5V and GND.
